Where the money comes from

Since 2024, about € 3.1 m. has gone into ME/CFS research in Vienna. Most of it is private money: from the WWTF, co-funded by the WE&ME Foundation, from donations and from Wiener Philharmonia.

Almost two thirds of it went to the Consolidation Call: two consortia, awarded in October 2026.
