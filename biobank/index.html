A biobank keeps samples from people with ME/CFS together with their health data, collected the same way every time. Research needs them to look for a biomarker: a measurable sign of the illness that would make diagnosis easier. Without stored samples, every study starts from zero.

The ME/CFS Biobank Austria is the first of its kind in Austria. Eva Untersmayr-Elsenhuber has led it at MedUni Wien since September 2023.

  • CollectPeople with a confirmed ME/CFS diagnosis give samples and fill in standard questionnaires. They come through the clinic and through the Austrian ME/CFS society.
  • StoreSamples and data follow the same protocols as the ME/CFS biobanks in Germany, the UK and the Netherlands.
  • ShareResearch projects can use them, in Vienna and with partner biobanks abroad. (Access tbc)

How the money came together

Until the end of 2025, the WE&ME Foundation paid for running the biobank, about € 60,000 a year. After that, nobody knew how it would go on. In November 2025, a gala in Vienna raised € 102,000. The WE&ME Foundation passed the donations to WWTF for the biobank, and the City of Vienna doubled them through matching funds. Two international experts reviewed the proposal.

€200,000Donations, doubled by the City of Vienna · first phase 18 months · interim evaluation 2027

Small start, larger network

Since June 2026, the biobank has been part of Discover-Me, an EU project that links ME/CFS biobanks across Europe. Its budget is € 7.5 m. (tbc)
