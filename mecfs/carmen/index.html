Her story

Carmen comes from Styria. She speaks four languages, lived in France twice and was studying for a master’s in transcultural communication. She did sport and travelled.

In 2022, although vaccinated, she fell ill with ME/CFS after COVID-19. She has not left the house since. Most of the time she lies alone in the dark. A visit of a few minutes from her mother Erika already costs her strength. On a good day she can take a bath with help, or write to friends.

There is no rehabilitation for people as ill as she is, and the few specialists, most of them private, are overloaded. From her bed, she writes about ME/CFS on X.

The decades of neglect have to do with the fact that most people with ME/CFS are young and female.
