What ME/CFS is

ME/CFS is a neuroimmunological multisystem disease, and so far there is no cure. It usually begins after an infection and is classified by the WHO under G93.3. Its key symptom is post-exertional malaise: after even small exertion, all symptoms get worse. At its most severe, people are bedbound.

In Austria, about 70,000 people have ME/CFS. Housebound or bedbound: share tbc.

Yet research has barely begun, because for a long time the disease received little attention. Before 2024 there was hardly any competitive funding: the FWF had funded one ME/CFS project.
