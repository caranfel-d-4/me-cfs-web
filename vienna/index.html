Today, many research groups in Vienna work on illnesses that follow an infection, ME/CFS among them. Funding made the field visible, and worth a career: young researchers came, and researchers from other fields.

What the field asks for

  1. Better data: a register, data donations.
  2. High-quality studies. The illness is too complex for small ones.
  3. New methods, and researchers from other fields with fresh eyes.
  4. ME/CFS research must not be a career dead end.
  5. Research developed together with the people who live with ME/CFS.
