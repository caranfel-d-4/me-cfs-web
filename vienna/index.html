Chapter: research in Vienna

Before 2024, there was no competitive funding for ME/CFS research in Vienna. The national research fund had funded a single ME/CFS project.

Today, about 30 research groups in Vienna work on illnesses that follow an infection, ME/CFS among them. Funding made the field visible, and worth a career: young researchers came, and researchers from other fields.

  • 1MedUni WienSix of the seven projects are led from here. Home of the ME/CFS Biobank Austria and of the national reference centre for post-viral syndromes.
  • 2TU WienGlycoExplore: sugar structures on proteins as markers of severity.
  • 3Ludwig Boltzmann Institutefor Rehabilitation Research. Partner in the study of muscle and heart metabolism.
  • 4GrazFH Joanneum and MedUni Graz, partners in two projects.
  • 5WWTFSchlickgasse 3, the funder.
  • 6WE&ME FoundationEiswerkstraße 18, co-funder.

What the field asks for

  1. Better data: a register, data donations.
  2. High-quality studies. The illness is too complex for small ones.
  3. New methods, and researchers from other fields with fresh eyes.
  4. ME/CFS research must not be a career dead end.
  5. Research developed together with the people who live with ME/CFS.
