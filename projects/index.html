7 exploratory projects

Call 2024

Seven small projects first. In 2024, the WWTF asked researchers in Vienna for exploratory projects on ME/CFS: up to € 100,000 each, for 12 to 15 months, to gather first data.

Seven were funded and started in January 2025. On what worked, a larger call builds: € 2 m., decided in October 2026.

19 proposals, 7 funded, € 695,445 in total.
Six projects are led from MedUni Wien, one from TU Wien.
→ 1 call to consolidate what worked
Source: WWTF, ME/CFS Call 2024
