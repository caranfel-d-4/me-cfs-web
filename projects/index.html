7 exploratory projects

Call 2024

Seven small projects first. In 2024, WWTF asked researchers in Vienna for exploratory projects on ME/CFS: up to € 100,000 each, for 12 to 15 months, to gather first data.

Seven were funded and started in January 2025. On what worked, a larger call builds: in October 2026, the leads of 002 and 018 won € 2 m. for two consortia.
