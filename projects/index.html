7 exploratory projects

WWTF’s ME/CFS 2024 – Understanding ME/CFS included 7 projects. Researchers in Vienna could apply for small exploratory projects: up to € 100,000 each, for 12 to 15 months, to gather first data.

The seven projects started in January 2025.

From many to few

In 2024, 19 proposals came in and 7 were funded. In 2026, two of them went further: in the Consolidation Call, the leads of 002 and 018 each won about € 1 m. for a consortium, to build on what worked.
