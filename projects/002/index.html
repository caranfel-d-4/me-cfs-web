The project looks for genes behind ME/CFS, with a focus on rare variants that change proteins. It analyses whole-genome data from biobanks such as UK Biobank and All of Us, and so adds to DecodeME, the large genetic study in the UK.

It also looks at long COVID. The results could help to sort patients into subgroups, and so to understand ME/CFS better.

Original title: “Genetic architecture of chronic fatigue syndrome”
